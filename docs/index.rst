How to Activate Peacock on Xfinity

1. Turn on your Xfinity device.
2. Open the Peacock app from the Apps menu.
3. Select **Sign In**.
4. An activation code may appear on your TV screen.
5. Using another device, open a web browser.
6. Visit the official Peacock activation page.
7. Enter the activation code exactly as displayed.
8. Sign in with your Peacock account if prompted.
9. Wait while your account is verified.
10. Your Xfinity device will refresh automatically, and you can begin streaming.

---------------------------

If your activation code isn't accepted:

* Verify that you entered the code correctly.
* Ensure the code has not expired.
* Restart the Peacock app to generate a new activation code.
* Enter the new code promptly.

Unable to Sign In
-----------------

If you cannot access your account:

* Verify your Peacock email address.
* Confirm your password.
* Reset your password if necessary.
* Ensure your Peacock subscription is active.

App Not Opening
---------------

If the Peacock app does not launch:

* Restart your Xfinity device.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
=================================

Do I need an activation code every time I use Peacock on Xfinity?
-----------------------------------------------------------------

No. An activation code is generally required only when signing in for the first time or after signing out of the Peacock app.

Can I use the same Peacock account on multiple devices?
-------------------------------------------------------

Yes. You can sign in to your Peacock account on multiple supported devices, subject to the simultaneous streaming limits associated with your subscription.

Why did my activation code expire?
----------------------------------

Activation codes are temporary for security reasons. If your code expires, reopen the Peacock app to receive a new one.

Can I sign in without an activation code?
-----------------------------------------

Yes. Depending on your Xfinity device and the Peacock app version, you may be able to sign in directly using your Peacock account credentials.

What should I do if activation still doesn't work?
--------------------------------------------------

Restart your Xfinity device, verify your internet connection, generate a new activation code, and ensure you're signing in with the correct Peacock account. If the issue continues, visit the official Peacock Help Center for additional assistance.
